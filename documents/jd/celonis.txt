THE ROLE

B2B SaaS environment. Focus on translating strategic needs into practical tools that enhance
operational efficiency and data quality.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  routing and content translation
- Develop hybrid solutions combining AI with human workflows

  custom AI solutions

  and data enrichment
- Partner with Brand and Communications on content generation and market trend analysis
- Measure business impact and optimize solution effectiveness
- Research and recommend emerging AI technologies

REQUIRED QUALIFICATIONS
- 5-7 years in development/engineering with production-level application experience
- Proficiency in Python or JavaScript with data manipulation libraries
- Hands-on experience with Langchain, Make, Zapier, or similar tools
- REST and GraphQL API development experience
- Deep understanding of LLMs and prompt engineering for business applications
- Product-oriented mindset with user-centric design focus
- Strong communication translating technical concepts for stakeholders

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS
- MarTech platform familiarity (Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo)
- B2B SaaS environment experience

NAMED TECHNOLOGIES
Python, JavaScript, Pandas, Langchain, Make, Zapier, REST, GraphQL, LLMs
